How do I play?

Tap a light to launch it the way its arrow points. It leaves the board if nothing is in its path; if something is, it bounces back and you lose a heart. Clear every light to win.

Is there a timer?

Only in Hard mode and the Daily Challenge. Easy and Normal are untimed.

My progress disappeared

Progress is stored on your phone only. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Delete your data

PHOTON has no accounts. The only data kept off your phone is your player name and scores on the public leaderboards. To delete them:

  1. Email zrngmehdi96@gmail.com with the subject "PHOTON data deletion".
  2. Include the player name shown in the app (menu → OPERATOR · NAME).
  3. We remove every leaderboard entry under that name within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Progress, stars and settings live only on your phone: uninstalling PHOTON deletes them. Data held by Google AdMob is managed through your Google account and your phone's ad settings.
