Privacy Policy · Last updated 26 September 2026

PHOTON ("the app") is a puzzle game published by Suli Sardam. This page explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

DataWhyWhere it goes
Player name you type in the appShown next to your score on the public leaderboardsGoogle Firebase (Cloud Firestore)
Scores (levels cleared; Daily Challenge result and date)Ranking on the leaderboardsGoogle Firebase (Cloud Firestore)
Advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location from IP addressServing the optional rewarded ads and measuring themGoogle AdMob

The player name and scores are public: anyone using the app can see them. Do not use your real name or any personal information as your player name.

The app does not ask for an account, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or microphone access.

Data kept only on your device

Your level progress, stars, streak, language, theme and sound settings are stored on your phone and never leave it. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Ads and consent

Ads in PHOTON are optional: one plays only when you choose to watch it for a reward (an extra life or a hint). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which processes data as described in Google's policy.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android; Settings → Privacy → Tracking on iOS).

Service providers

  • Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore — stores the leaderboards. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob — serves the optional ads. policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell your data.

Children

PHOTON is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
